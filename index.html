The map

Istanbul to Transylvania

The night train to Bucharest is closed in 2027

The rail bridge between Bulgaria and Romania (Giurgiu–Ruse) is shut for construction from 5 April to 13 December 2027. That is the only normal train into Bucharest. Do not buy an Istanbul-to-Bucharest sleeper off a 2026 timetable and hope it still exists.

Real plan: night train Istanbul → Sofia (that one still runs every day), then a van into Romania. Backup if the train sells out: fly Istanbul → Bucharest.

The week

What we do each day

Open the week
  1. 01Tue, Sep 14, 2027Leave IAD after darkMeet at Dulles in the evening. Fly to Istanbul overnight. Sleep on the plane.
  2. 02Wed, Sep 15, 2027Land, water, first tableLand in Istanbul in the afternoon. One van to the apartment. Walk the neighborhood. Dinner. No museums tonight.
  3. 03Thu, Sep 16, 2027The old city, onceHagia Sophia when it opens. Then the underground cistern. One more museum only if you still have legs. Long trousers all day.
  4. 04Fri, Sep 17, 2027Hammam and the straitBoat on the Bosphorus in the morning. Turkish bath at 4:30pm — not 9am. Dinner. Pack for the train tomorrow.
  5. 05Sat, Sep 18, 2027The countryside sleeperEasy morning. Buy food for the train. Leave the apartment at 4pm. Night train at 8pm. There is no restaurant on board.
  6. 06Sun, Sep 19, 2027Into RomaniaWake up in Sofia. Coffee. Do not tour Sofia. Van to Romania. Sleep in Brașov if you arrive before dark. Bucharest if you don't.
  7. 07Mon, Sep 20, 2027Bran for the silhouette, Brașov for the townBran Castle (the postcard), then Râșnov (the better ruin), then Brașov town. It is a Monday — the castle may not open until noon.
  8. 08Tue, Sep 21, 2027Poenari — the real citadelLeave at 7am. Three-hour drive. Climb 1,480 steps to Vlad's actual fortress. Drive home before dark.
  9. 09Wed, Sep 22, 2027Under the mountain, then a palaceSalt mine in the morning — bring a sweater. Palace on the way home in the afternoon.
  10. 10Thu, Sep 23, 2027Spare morning, then southLast coffee in Brașov. Drive or train to Bucharest. No more castles.
  11. 11Fri, Sep 24, 2027Last night in the capitalBucharest Old Town. One dinner. The flight is tomorrow morning.
  12. 12Sat, Sep 25, 2027WestboundAirport. Fly home through Istanbul. Same ticket as the way out.
