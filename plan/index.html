Project Bromania MMXXVII

Day by day

15–25 Sep 2027. Night train via Sofia. Each day has two lines at the top: what you are doing, and whose job it is. If you only read those, you will still make the train.

You lose one Transylvania day compared with flying. Bran, Poenari, and the salt mine still fit.

  1. Airborne

    Day 01 · Tue, Sep 14, 2027 · Dulles

    Leave IAD after dark

    Sleep: TK8, 21:40

    Today

    Meet at Dulles in the evening. Fly to Istanbul overnight. Sleep on the plane.

    Your job

    Admin buys one ticket for all five of you. Everyone: passport, one suitcase, long trousers. Eat at the airport — dinner in Istanbul will be late.

    Why this day is on the trip

    Every trip like this starts in a fluorescent terminal, not a fortress. Five names on one ticket, bags together, a meal at the airport because Istanbul dinner will be late. Tonight is for sleeping on the plane. The rakı starts on the ground.

    1. 18:30

      Turkish check-in, Terminal 1

      Why

      One ticket, five men, no one left at security with a separate confirmation number. That is how a crew actually leaves a country.

      Do this

      Turkish Airlines, flight TK8. One booking for all five. Bags together. Eat here.

    2. 21:40

      Wheels up

      Why

      Ten hours of nothing is a feature. You land Wednesday afternoon with a whole first night still in front of you.

      Do this

      Sleep. Water. Do not start the trip on the plane.

  2. Istanbul

    Day 02 · Wed, Sep 15, 2027 · Istanbul

    Land, water, first table

    Sleep: Galata / Karaköy

    Today

    Land in Istanbul in the afternoon. One van to the apartment. Walk the neighborhood. Dinner. No museums tonight.

    Your job

    Admin: apartment and airport van already booked. Everyone: do not take five separate taxis.

    Why this day is on the trip

    First night is not for monuments. It is for the neighborhood you will actually live in: the water, a roof, a table that does not close at nine, and five chairs that belong to you. Sultanahmet after dark is a postcard with the lights off. Karaköy is still a city.

    1. 14:50

      IST, then the van

      Why

      A boys trip that starts with five separate taxi negotiations is already behind. One van, one drop, one apartment.

      Do this

      One pre-booked van, or the Havaist bus. 60–90 minutes. Buy a phone SIM at the airport only if nobody set one up.

    2. 17:00

      Walk Galata

      Why

      This is the street you will come home to for three nights — tower, lanes, the Horn at the bottom of the hill. You are not sightseeing yet. You are locating the bar.

      Do this

      Tower street and Karaköy lanes. Skip the tower queue unless it is short. The view is better from a roof with a drink.

    3. 19:00

      Roof, then a real meyhane

      Why

      Istanbul's drinking culture is meze and rakı at a table you do not leave for three hours — not a rooftop tourist grill with a DJ. This is the first night the five of you are actually on the trip.

      Do this

      Bank Roof or Georges for the view. Then Asmalımescit or Karaköy Lokantası. White cheese, grilled liver, no belly-dance package. Curfew 23:30. Jet lag is undefeated.

  3. Istanbul

    Day 03 · Thu, Sep 16, 2027 · Istanbul

    The old city, once

    Sleep: Galata / Karaköy

    Today

    Hagia Sophia when it opens. Then the underground cistern. One more museum only if you still have legs. Long trousers all day.

    Your job

    Admin: buy Hagia Sophia and cistern tickets before this morning. Everyone: shoulders covered, downstairs by 8am.

    Why this day is on the trip

    You get one day in the old city. Do it at opening, in long trousers, and then leave. The mistake is stacking three mosques, the Grand Bazaar, and Topkapı until everyone hates Istanbul. The move is Hagia Sophia, the Cistern, one more thing, and a sunset that is not from a tour bus.

    1. 08:30

      Hagia Sophia

      Why

      Fifteen hundred years of church, mosque, museum, mosque. You walk into the volume of it — the dome, the light, the fact that this building outlasted the empires that built it. That is the one monument that earns a boys-trip morning.

      Do this

      Be there when it opens. Long trousers, shoulders covered. Official gallery ticket is €25 — the museum pass does not work here. Blue Mosque courtyard if it is open. Do not add a third mosque.

    2. 11:00

      Basilica Cistern

      Why

      Underground Istanbul. Columns in black water, Medusa heads used as column bases, the cinematic one from every film that needed a secret city. It is twenty minutes of actually-cool.

      Do this

      Buy on yerebatan.com or Passo, or at the window that morning. Never a website named basilica-cistern.com. Skip the audio guide. Walk it.

    3. 13:00

      Topkapı or the Archaeological Museum

      Why

      Topkapı if anyone wants weapons, relics, and the imperial treasury. The Archaeological Museum if you would rather see the actual objects without the palace crush. Pick one. The Grand Bazaar is a 45-minute pass, then you leave — Spice Bazaar is the better shop.

      Do this

      One palace or museum. Grand Bazaar as a shortcut, not a destination.

    4. 17:30

      Süleymaniye at sunset

      Why

      The Golden Horn from a working mosque courtyard, not a paid terrace. Locals, students, the city actually happening. Dinner back on the nightlife side of the water.

      Do this

      Walk or tram. Do not eat in Sultanahmet unless legs are done.

  4. Istanbul

    Day 04 · Fri, Sep 17, 2027 · Istanbul

    Hammam and the strait

    Sleep: Galata / Karaköy

    Today

    Boat on the Bosphorus in the morning. Turkish bath at 4:30pm — not 9am. Dinner. Pack for the train tomorrow.

    Your job

    Admin: book the bath for five men after 4:30pm. Everyone: spare underwear for the bath.

    Why this day is on the trip

    This is the Istanbul day that is not a museum. A 16th-century bath, then the water the whole trip is named after. The Bosporus Express cannot run to Bucharest in 2027. The Bosporus itself is still here. You take a boat. That is how you keep the promise.

    1. 10:00

      Two hours on the Bosporus

      Why

      Europe on one bank, Asia on the other, palaces, fortresses, the current pulling you north. This is the water the night train was supposed to leave from. A private boat from Karaköy, or the public ferry to Anadolu Kavağı if you want to do it the old way. Either is the point. Skipping it because the train died is missing the trip.

      Do this

      Public ferry from Karaköy, or a private boat for two hours. Bring a jacket. No dinner-cruise with a DJ. The bath is tonight at 4:30pm.

    2. 16:30

      Kılıç Ali Paşa Hamam

      Why

      Sinan built this bath in 1580 for a pirate admiral. Marble, steam, a very serious man with a mitt. Five of you in a 16th-century room getting reset after the strait — that is a dude-trip ritual, not a spa day.

      Do this

      Men only from 4:30pm to 11:30pm. Do not book 9am — that is women’s hours. Book five together on kilicalipasahamami.com. Walk from the apartment. Tip in Turkish lira.

    3. 20:00

      Last Karaköy table

      Why

      Steam, then a table you do not leave. Tomorrow is the sleeper. Tonight is still Istanbul.

      Do this

      A real meyhane in Karaköy. Pack the apartment after dinner. Buy train food tomorrow morning, not at 11pm.

  5. The crossing

    Day 05 · Sat, Sep 18, 2027 · Halkalı → Sofia

    The countryside sleeper

    Sleep: Three 2-berth TVS2000s

    Today

    Easy morning. Buy food for the train. Leave the apartment at 4pm. Night train at 8pm. There is no restaurant on board.

    Your job

    Admin: train tickets already bought. Everyone: help buy the picnic, passport in your pocket, do not be late for 4pm.

    Why this day is on the trip

    This is the night the trip is named after. The Bosporus Express cannot run its usual road to Bucharest in 2027 — Friendship Bridge is closed April to December — but the Istanbul–Sofia Express still leaves Halkalı at 20:00, every day of the year. Thrace in the dark. A border stamp around 04:00. You wake up in the Bulgarian valley: villages, Plovdiv, autumn fields. Five men in lockable compartments, a picnic, no dining car. That is a countryside sleeper. It is not a luxury train. It is the real one.

    1. 10:00

      Biennial, or a last Karaköy morning

      Why

      Saturday 18 Sep is the Biennial's public opening — actual contemporary art in a city that is not only mosques. One pavilion is enough. If the crew would rather eat, Karaköy still has a table.

      Do this

      One pavilion, not a museum marathon. Then buy the train food. Bags out of the apartment at 4pm.

    2. 16:00

      Marmaray to Halkalı

      Why

      Halkalı is not Sirkeci. The romance station is gone. The working station is 45–70 minutes west, and missing it is how you lose the night.

      Do this

      Leave the apartment by 4pm. Food already bought in Karaköy or the Spice Bazaar. The train has no restaurant. The station at Halkalı is not a grocery store.

    3. 20:00

      Istanbul–Sofia Express

      Why

      Three 2-berth sleepers for five large Americans: two sharing, two sharing, one sole-use. Lockable doors, a sink, a mini-fridge, lower berths for the heaviest. This is the closest living cousin of the train you wanted, and it actually exists in 2027.

      Do this

      Bought on TCDD about eight weeks before (July 2027). Names as on passports. About €48 each. Heavier guys get the lower beds.

    4. 04:00

      The border

      Why

      You may be asked off at Edirne, not Kapıkule. Passports in one envelope per compartment. One man half-awake. The 4am stamp is part of the story, not a hassle to optimize away.

      Do this

      Passports ready. Valuables stay on you. Then sleep until morning.

  6. The crossing

    Into Romania

    Sleep: Brașov if you make it; Lipscani if you don't

    Wake up in Sofia. Coffee. Do not tour Sofia. Van to Romania. Sleep in Brașov if you arrive before dark. Bucharest if you don't.

    Your job

    Admin: van is booked from Sofia station. Everyone: stay with the group. Do not wander off to see a church.

    Why this day is on the trip

    You land in Sofia around noon with a whole afternoon to spend on purpose. Do not 'do Sofia.' Coffee, banitsa, then a van through the countryside to Bucharest. Five hours, you control the stops, same spirit as the train. If you roll in after 19:00, sleep in Lipscani and drive north in the morning. The mountain days start when you are in Brașov, not when you are heroic on DN1 in the dark.

    1. 11:00

      Sofia station

      Why

      A capital you are passing through, not collecting. The win is being on time for the van, not a church.

      Do this

      Coffee and pastry near the station. Stretch. Do not check bags into a locker for a 'quick look.'

    2. 13:00

      Van, Sofia to Bucharest

      Why

      The Friendship Bridge is closed all of 2027, so the train cannot finish the job. A pre-booked van for five is the honest sequel: countryside, border, Wallachia, you decide when to stop.

      Do this

      Pre-booked van for five, about five hours. A bus is the backup. Fly only if everyone refuses more sitting.

    3. evening

      North to Brașov — or stop

      Why

      Brașov is base camp. Getting there shattered helps nobody. A Lipscani night is not a failure. It is arriving like adults.

      Do this

      If you hit Bucharest by 5pm, keep going to Brașov (2.5–3 hours). After 7pm, hotel in Old Town, drive north at 8am.

  7. Transylvania

    Day 07 · Mon, Sep 20, 2027 · Brașov

    Bran for the silhouette, Brașov for the town

    Sleep: Brașov old town

    Today

    Bran Castle (the postcard), then Râșnov (the better ruin), then Brașov town. It is a Monday — the castle may not open until noon.

    Your job

    Admin: Bran tickets, or cash at the gate. Driver: out on time. Everyone: no Dracula dinner show.

    Why this day is on the trip

    Bran is the castle everyone already has in their head — the turrets, the cliff, the Dracula merch. The historical link to Vlad Țepeș is thin. You still go, because the silhouette is the silhouette, and doing it at 8:45 means you get the building without the mall. Then Râșnov, which is the better ruin: a peasant fortress on a ridge, a view, almost no kitsch. Afternoon is Brașov itself — a Saxon square you can actually drink in, which is why this town is base camp and not a day trip from Bucharest.

    1. 08:00

      Bran Castle

      Why

      This is the Hollywood castle. Queen Marie's interiors, a courtyard, the shot. You give it 90 minutes because it is the image the trip will be asked about, and then you leave before the coaches turn it into a queue. It is not Vlad's house. Tomorrow is.

      Do this

      Monday 20 Sep. Bran has often opened at noon on Mondays, 9am the rest of the week. Check bran-castle.com. If noon, do Râșnov first. 90 minutes inside. Râșnov is ten minutes up the road.

    2. 14:00

      Brașov old town

      Why

      A real medieval square that still functions as a town: Black Church, rope-walk streets, Tampa mountain over the roofs. This is where you will eat for four nights. Walk it so the square is yours, not a backdrop.

      Do this

      Black Church, the lanes, cable car up Tampa if you want the view. Early dinner. Tomorrow starts at 7am.

    3. 20:00

      Square table

      Why

      Transylvanian food — ciorbă, smoked meats, pickles, palinca — not a Dracula dinner show. The show is five chairs on Piața Sfatului.

      Do this

      Skip anything with a cape on the menu.

  8. Transylvania

    Day 08 · Tue, Sep 21, 2027 · Arefu

    Poenari — the real citadel

    Sleep: Brașov

    Today

    Leave at 7am. Three-hour drive. Climb 1,480 steps to Vlad's actual fortress. Drive home before dark.

    Your job

    Admin: small cash in lei at the steps. Everyone: boots, water, no racing. Do not add the high mountain road — you would get back at midnight.

    Why this day is on the trip

    Poenari is not a castle tour. It is a cliff. In the 1450s Vlad Țepeș — the Wallachian voivode Bram Stoker later borrowed, the man who impaled Ottoman prisoners on the road to Târgoviște — rebuilt this citadel as a mountain stronghold against the empire to the south. He did not live at Bran. He used Poenari. The Ottomans wrecked it. What is left is broken wall, wind, and a drop into the Argeș gorge. You climb 1,480 concrete steps cut into the rock. Forty-five minutes up. No gift-shop gauntlet first. At the top: a ruin five men in their forties just walked up to, on a cliff the Impaler actually held. That is the dude-trip photograph. Bran was the postcard. This is the story.

    1. 07:00

      The drive

      Why

      Three hours of Wallachian road — hills, villages, then the gorge. The low road keeps you on time. The Transfăgărășan roof from Sibiu is a different day and a different trip. Do not stack them.

      Do this

      The eight-seater van. Low road toward Câmpulung / Curtea de Argeș / Arefu. About three hours. Water in the van. Out of Brașov at 7:00, not 7:40.

    2. 10:00

      1,480 steps to Vlad's house

      Why

      This is the whole point of Romania on this trip. A working military citadel on a needle of rock. You feel the height. You see why it was useful. You stand in a courtyard that was a fortress, not a museum reconstruction. Folklore says a woman of Vlad's court leapt from these walls rather than be taken — that is a story. The citadel is not. Five moderately fit guys walking up it in September is the adventure. There is no ride, no tram, no skip-the-line. The steps are the ticket.

      Do this

      Park at the bottom. Pay at the booth — about 30 lei, cash lei as backup. 45–60 minutes up, 45 on the walls, same way down. Water. Boots, not gym shoes. The ruin is small. The cliff is the point.

    3. 12:30

      Vidraru Dam

      Why

      Twenty minutes up the gorge: a 1960s arch dam, a lake, the road clinging to the rock. After a medieval ruin it is a jolt — concrete, scale, the 20th century answering the 15th. Lunch in Arefu, same road home, Brașov before dark.

      Do this

      Walk the dam if the weather is clean. Do not add the Transfăgărășan crossing. You would get back at midnight.

  9. Transylvania

    Day 09 · Wed, Sep 22, 2027 · Slănic

    Under the mountain, then a palace

    Sleep: Brașov

    Today

    Salt mine in the morning — bring a sweater. Palace on the way home in the afternoon.

    Your job

    Admin: Peleș tickets already bought for the afternoon, not 9am. Everyone: sweater. Cash at the mine window.

    Why this day is on the trip

    Unirea at Slănic Prahova is a stadium hollowed out of salt, 208 meters down, air at 13–16°C. A ferris wheel, a football pitch, the walls glittering. It looks like a set. Then, on the corridor home, Peleș Castle in Sinaia: a 19th-century royal pile of carved wood, armor, and leftover empire, the opposite of Poenari's ruin and equally unhinged. Two kinds of extra on one day.

    1. 09:30

      Salina Unirea

      Why

      You are standing in a room the size of an arena that used to be a mountain. People came down here as a cure. You come down because it is one of the strangest interiors in the country, and because Turda — the prettier theme-park mine — is five hours away and would kill this itinerary. Slănic fits. It is also cold, which after Poenari's steps is a gift.

      Do this

      Sweater. Buy tickets at the window, about 60 lei. 90 minutes underground is enough. Closed Mondays. Wednesday 22 Sep is a good day. Last bus up is mid-afternoon — leave time for the palace.

    2. 14:00

      Peleș Castle, Sinaia

      Why

      King Carol I built a German-fantasy palace in the Carpathians and filled it like a man who had just discovered catalogs. Armor halls, carved ceilings, a last-century flex. After a salt cathedral and a cliff ruin, this is the third castle of the week and the only one that looks like money. Worth 75 minutes. Not worth a separate day.

      Do this

      Buy on bilete.peles.ro, afternoon slot (2pm–3pm). Closed Monday and Tuesday. Last entry 4pm. Skip the little castle next door.

  11. The capital
