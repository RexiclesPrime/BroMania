The night train to Bucharest is closed in 2027

The rail bridge between Bulgaria and Romania (Giurgiu–Ruse) is shut for construction from 5 April to 13 December 2027. That is the only normal train into Bucharest. Do not buy an Istanbul-to-Bucharest sleeper off a 2026 timetable and hope it still exists.

Real plan: night train Istanbul → Sofia (that one still runs every day), then a van into Romania. Backup if the train sells out: fly Istanbul → Bucharest.
